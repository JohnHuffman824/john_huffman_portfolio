About Me

I played varsity football at Carleton College while earning a BA in Computer Science with a minor in Mathematics (Class of 2024, 3.7 GPA). After graduating, I stayed on as an assistant coach — first at Carleton, then at Bishop Garcia Diego High School in Santa Barbara. Coaching taught me that the tools available to small programs are terrible: clunky playbook apps designed for the NFL, not for a coach drawing plays at midnight before a Saturday game. So I built Play-Smith.

Now I'm a full-stack developer at Spider Strategies, where I lead development of Impact Assistant — an AI chatbot for enterprise performance management. I work across JavaScript, TypeScript, and Kotlin, building software used by government agencies and enterprises. Outside work, I keep building tools at the intersection of software and domains I care about. Based in Austin, TX.
